# Contributing

**Bug reports:** open an issue with your operating system, desktop environment
or Windows version, the smabar version, and the relevant lines from
`~/.smabar/logs/`.

**Pull requests:** `just check` must pass, and source files stay under 500
lines. Keep the change focused; describe what you saw and what changed.

**Licensing of contributions:** by submitting a contribution you license it
under the license of the directory it lands in — PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 for the
repository, MIT for `sdk/` and `plugins/` — and you grant the maintainer the
right to relicense it together with the project.
